# Golden 08 — The alley (local "history" test)

**Genre/tone:** suspense, Victorian city. Invented text.
**Period:** London, 1888 (stated in the first sentence).

## Brano

London, November 1888. Greta left the shop late and walked home along the river. The
street was almost empty and her boots were wet. At the corner she stopped, zipped up her
coat and looked back. A man was standing under the arch, not moving. She walked faster,
counting the doors until her own, and only when the key turned did she breathe again.

## Notes

Two hooks on purpose: a possible period detail (the street, the evening) and an
anachronism (a zip on a coat in 1888).

After the runs only the character name changed, to follow the agreed name table.

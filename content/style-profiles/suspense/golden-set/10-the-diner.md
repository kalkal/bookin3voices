# Golden 10 — The diner (history test, not tuned)

**Genre/tone:** quiet dread, small American town. Invented text.
**Period:** Ohio, 1925 (stated in the first sentence).

## Brano

Ohio, spring of 1925. The diner was empty except for a man in a grey hat at the counter.
Lidia poured his coffee and went back to the kitchen, where she put his pie in the
microwave and waited. When she came out again the stool was empty and the coffee was
still steaming, as if no one had touched it.

## Notes

(a microwave oven in 1925) and hooks for a period detail (the diner, the counter, the hat).

# Golden 12 — The station (history check, never seen)

**Genre/tone:** quiet unease, a city at the turn of the century. Invented text.
**Period:** Paris, 1900 (stated in the first sentence).

## Brano

Paris, spring of 1900. The station hall smelled of coal smoke and wet wool. Silvia
waited by the clock with her small case, and when the man in the brown coat came at
last she showed him her boarding pass for the morning flight to London. He read it
twice, folded it, and kept it.

## Notes

Written BEFORE any run and never edited: one anachronism (a boarding pass for a flight
in 1900), hooks for a period detail (the station, the coal smoke, the clock).
